AI Failure Prediction Interface
Overview
01
Problem
$120 billion in flood-related damages every year. Cities face water crises without the tools to simulate or prepare. Leaks, floods, and pipe failures cause service losses especially for hospitals, schools, and vulnerable populations.
02
Solution
A smart, interactive platform that models the city's water system as a digital twin — powered by graph neural networks. WA:SIM simulates damage from floods and failures, predicts flow and pressure, prioritises repairs, and works with incomplete sensor coverage.
03
Role

04
How It Works
Graph model: nodes are junctions and demand zones; edges are pipes. A GNN learns how water flows even in missing-data conditions. The simulation engine tests floods, pipe cuts, or rising demand. Dashboard insights highlight vulnerable zones and planning priorities.
05
Impact
Prioritised maintenance, fairer access to water, sustainable infrastructure. City planners test what-if crises before they happen. Hospitals and schools stay protected during system failures. Aligned with EU and SDG6 goals.
06
What's Next
Finalise simulation proof-of-concept. Connect to real-time sensor data via IoT. Build partnerships with city governments.

01
3D as Data
The city isn't decoration — it's the data. Switching between 2D overhead and 3D isometric gives operators two mental models: network topology and physical reality. Both are necessary.
02
Signal, Not Style
High-contrast neon on near-black reduces cognitive load in field conditions. Cyan for navigation, magenta for active states, red for critical alerts — each hue carries semantic meaning.
03
Voice-First
When operators are wearing gloves or managing physical systems, touch is unreliable. The microphone button anchors every screen — the interface is designed to be operated without looking.
04
Map · Predict · Failure Zones
Three core modes moving between a bird's-eye network view and granular street-level analysis. One tap activates the AI prediction layer.
